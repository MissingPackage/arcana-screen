Privacy and local data

ArcanaScreen is local-first. The app does not require an account and does not transmit screen, note, encounter, dice or timer data to an ArcanaScreen service.

What is stored

Your screens, tool state, preferences and recovery snapshots are stored in this browser using local storage. They remain on this device and browser profile unless you export them or clear browser data.

Telemetry

No analytics, advertising SDK, tracking pixel or automatic error-reporting service is included. Future telemetry must be off by default and require explicit consent before any data leaves the browser.

Backups and deletion

Data & recovery can download a portable JSON backup. You choose where that file is stored and whether to share it. The same panel can reset all ArcanaScreen data in the current browser after confirmation.

External links

Opening a rules source or the feedback form takes you to another website. That site applies its own privacy policy and can receive normal request metadata such as your IP address and browser information.

Questions and feedback

Report a privacy concern through the ArcanaScreen issue tracker. Do not include private campaign data in a public issue.
